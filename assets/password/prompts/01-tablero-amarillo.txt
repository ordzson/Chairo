Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity portrait mobile game screen concept, 941 x 1672 proportions
Primary request: Design the active gameplay screen for the Chairo Bible party game “Password”. This is a real app UI screen, not a poster. A participant holds this phone against their forehead and cannot see the word, while the other participant sees it and gives clues. Preserve the reference image’s Chairo identity: cream textured mission-binder paper, blue binder spine and brass rings on the left, saturated royal blue ink, joyful yellow and orange molded plastic, rounded tactile 2000s educational-game depth, hand-painted underline marks, the same logo and typography character. Do not reuse the reference screen’s quote or answer buttons.
Screen structure from top to bottom: compact Chairo logo at top; a slim blue game header reading “PASSWORD” and small status “Ronda 3”; a huge central yellow molded word tile occupying most of the screen with the single word “JERICÓ” in extremely large dark-blue uppercase letters, readable at arm’s length; a tiny handwritten hint under it “Da pistas, no digas la palabra”; bottom controls stay calm and secondary. Put a small circular countdown timer only in the top-right corner of the play area, same molded ring style as the reference timer, showing “24”, with no giant countdown. Add a small blue status label near the bottom “Teléfono de Ana” and one wide orange molded host-only button reading “TERMINAR RONDA”.
Composition/framing: strict portrait phone UI, edge-to-edge screen, one dominant word surface, generous margins, no device mockup, no people, no scenery.
Text (verbatim): “PASSWORD”, “Ronda 3”, “JERICÓ”, “Da pistas, no digas la palabra”, “24”, “Teléfono de Ana”, “TERMINAR RONDA”.
Constraints: Spanish text only; the word must be the unmistakable focal point; timer stays in one corner and never competes with the word; all controls must look implementable as HTML/CSS; accessible high contrast; preserve the binder material language and exact blue-yellow-cream brand family.
Avoid: scoreboard, points, question cards, answer choices, QR code, gradients unrelated to molded plastic, glassmorphism, generic modern dashboard, photographs, hands, phone frame, watermark, illegible or extra text.
