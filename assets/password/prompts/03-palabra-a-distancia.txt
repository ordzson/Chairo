Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: third high-fidelity portrait mobile UI concept for Chairo Password gameplay
Primary request: Design the most distilled, distance-readable version of the active “Password” gameplay screen. It is still unmistakably Chairo and still a functional app UI. The phone is held against the participant’s forehead, so the other player must read the word instantly. Preserve the reference image’s established Chairo visual world: tactile mission binder, cream fibrous paper around the edges, blue spine with brass rings on the left, royal-blue ink, yellow and orange molded-plastic pieces, playful bold condensed lettering, hand-painted accent marks, early-2000s educational-game polish. Do not reuse the reference screen’s quote, scoreboard, progress dots, or answer choices.
Composition: very compact top strip with small Chairo mark and “PASSWORD”. Below it, one enormous deep-blue molded panel takes roughly 70 percent of the screen height, almost edge to edge. Center a single word “GOLIAT” in huge cream uppercase letters on that blue panel, with a loose yellow painted underline; no other content inside the focal area. Embed a small yellow circular countdown in the upper-right corner of the blue panel showing “24”, styled exactly like the app’s established countdown but only about one seventh of panel width. At the bottom on cream paper, show a small handwritten instruction “Ayuda a tu compañero a adivinar” and a secondary orange host-only button “TERMINAR RONDA”. Add a tiny label “Ronda 3 · Ana” above the button. This option should feel calmer and more functional than decorative.
Text (verbatim): “PASSWORD”, “GOLIAT”, “24”, “Ayuda a tu compañero a adivinar”, “Ronda 3 · Ana”, “TERMINAR RONDA”.
Constraints: portrait 941 x 1672 proportions, edge-to-edge real UI screen, no device frame, maximal word legibility, timer confined to one corner, accessible contrast, Spanish only, all UI translatable to semantic HTML/CSS.
Avoid: giant timer, score, points, player cards, multiple choice, QR, photographs, people, hands, game-show stage, generic dashboard, glassmorphism, extra copy, watermark, illegible letters.
